Kaleo Arts Privacy Policy & SMS Messaging Terms and Conditions

Kaleo Arts (“Kaleo Arts,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy and SMS Messaging Terms and Conditions explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we handle information provided when you sign up to receive text messages from Kaleo Arts.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Student and parent/guardian information
  • Class, camp, lesson, and registration information
  • Payment and account information
  • Information you provide when contacting us or registering for our programs, classes, camps, events, or private lessons

We may also collect limited information about how you interact with our website, registration systems, emails, and text messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and manage Kaleo Arts programs and to communicate with families and customers.

This may include using your information to:

  • Process registrations and manage student accounts
  • Manage classes, camps, private lessons, and events
  • Process payments
  • Send class, lesson, camp, and event reminders
  • Send schedule, location, rehearsal, performance, and program updates
  • Communicate important administrative information
  • Respond to questions and customer service requests
  • Send SMS notifications that you have agreed to receive
  • Send promotional or marketing communications when you have provided appropriate consent
  • Improve our programs, services, and communications

We do not sell your personal information.

3. SMS/Text Messaging Program

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in to receive text messages from Kaleo Arts, you agree to receive SMS messages related to your relationship with Kaleo Arts.

Depending on your preferences and your relationship with our programs, messages may include:

  • Class and lesson reminders
  • Schedule and location changes
  • Registration and enrollment information
  • Camp and event information
  • Performance and rehearsal information
  • Important program announcements
  • Administrative or account notifications
  • Promotional messages about Kaleo Arts programs, when applicable

Message frequency may vary.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Kaleo Arts.

4. How to Opt Out

You may stop receiving SMS messages from Kaleo Arts at any time by replying STOP to any text message from us.

After you send STOP, you may receive one additional message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After that, you will no longer receive SMS messages unless you separately opt in again.

You may also contact Kaleo Arts directly to request removal from our messaging program.

6. Sharing of Information

Kaleo Arts does not sell or rent your personal information, mobile phone number, or SMS consent information.

We do not sell, rent, transfer, or otherwise disclose consumer data, including SMS consent information, to any external organizations except service providers acting on our behalf to operate the messaging program, and never for those organizations’ own use or marketing purposes.

We may use service providers that help us operate our business and provide services to you. These may include providers that assist with student registration, payment processing, website services, email communication, SMS/text messaging, and other business operations.

Service providers may receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform services on behalf of Kaleo Arts. They are permitted to use this information only to provide the services we have engaged them to perform and are not permitted to use consumer data, mobile phone numbers, or SMS consent information for their own independent use or marketing purposes.

We do not share SMS consent information with external organizations for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal processes, or valid governmental requests, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Kaleo Arts, our customers, students, families, or others.

7. SMS Consent Information

Your consent to receive SMS messages is maintained as part of our messaging program. SMS consent information, including your mobile phone number and opt-in status, is used to administer and operate the Kaleo Arts messaging program.

SMS consent information will not be sold, rented, transferred, or otherwise disclosed to external organizations except to service providers acting on behalf of Kaleo Arts as necessary to operate the messaging program. Such service providers may not use SMS consent information for their own independent purposes or marketing purposes.

Kaleo Arts does not share SMS consent information with external organizations for those organizations’ own marketing purposes.

8. Data Security

Kaleo Arts takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

We limit access to personal information to individuals and service providers who need the information to perform legitimate business functions.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored electronically.

9. Children's Privacy

Kaleo Arts provides programs for children and teens. Information about students is generally provided by a parent or legal guardian and is collected for purposes related to registration, participation, safety, communication, and administration of our programs.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information for unrelated marketing purposes.

Their handling of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies and applicable laws.
