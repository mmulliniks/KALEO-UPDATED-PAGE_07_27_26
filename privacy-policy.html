Kaleo Arts Privacy Policy & SMS Messaging Terms and Conditions

Kaleo Arts (“Kaleo Arts,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy and SMS Messaging Terms and Conditions explains how we collect and use information, including information provided when you sign up to receive text messages from Kaleo Arts.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Student and parent/guardian information
  • Registration and class information
  • Payment and account information
  • Information you provide when contacting us or registering for programs, camps, classes, events, or private lessons

We may also collect information about how you interact with our website, registration systems, emails, and text messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

Kaleo Arts may use your information to:

  • Register and manage students for classes, camps, lessons, and events
  • Communicate with parents, guardians, students, and families about programs and registrations
  • Send important reminders and updates regarding classes, rehearsals, performances, schedule changes, closures, and events
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Process payments and manage accounts
  • Send promotional and marketing communications when you have provided appropriate consent
  • Improve our programs, services, and communications

3. SMS/Text Messaging Program

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to receive text messages from Kaleo Arts, you agree to receive text messages from us regarding your relationship with Kaleo Arts.

Messages may include:

  • Class and lesson reminders
  • Schedule and location updates
  • Registration and enrollment information
  • Performance and event information
  • Camp information
  • Important program announcements
  • Account or administrative notifications
  • Promotional offers and information about Kaleo Arts programs, when applicable

Message frequency may vary.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Kaleo Arts.

4. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving text messages from Kaleo Arts at any time by replying STOP to a text message from us.

After you send STOP, you may receive one additional message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After that, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us unless you separately opt in again.

You may also contact Kaleo Arts directly to request removal from our text messaging program.

SMS opt-in information and consent will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

8. Cookies and Website Information

Our website and third-party services we use may collect certain information automatically, such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, and general usage information. Cookies and similar technologies may be used to improve website functionality and understand website usage.

9. Children's Privacy

Kaleo Arts provides programs for children and teens. We collect information about students primarily from their parents or legal guardians for purposes related to registration, program participation, safety, communication, and administration.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information for unrelated marketing purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

Kaleo Arts may use third-party platforms and service providers for registration, payment processing, email communication, text messaging, website hosting, and other business functions. These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.
