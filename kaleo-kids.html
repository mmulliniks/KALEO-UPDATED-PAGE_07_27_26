Plate 04 · The class

Four classes in one.

Every Kaleo Kids session rotates through three disciplines, plus a weekly community time lesson.

i · Singing

Singing.

Finding their unique voice, learning to sing, and rehearsing music for the show.

ii · Dancing

Dancing.

Learning basic dance technique, finding rhythm, and rehearsing choreography built for the show.

iii · Acting

Acting.

Discovering how to express emotion, build speaking skills, and bringing their character to life, line by line.

iv · Community

Community time.

A weekly lesson built around character, not just craft - using our 8 Kaleo Values.

All in one hour and forty-five minutes, one day a week.

Plate 07 · Origin story
Renee Mulliniks · Founder

Where Kaleo
began.

Kaleo Kids is where Kaleo Arts began. Since our first production in 2005, we've had hundreds of performances and thousands of young performers under our lights.

Kaleo Kids is led by Renee Mulliniks, who founded the program as a simple experiment: could kids who'd never set foot on a stage pull off an original production? Two decades later, it's Kaleo's flagship.
