Plate 05 · Why one-on-one
Focused, personal instruction

Not every kid learns best in a room of twenty.

Group classes are wonderful — they build community, camaraderie, and the joy of performing together. But some students learn fastest with focused attention on the specifics of their instrument or voice.

Private Lessons give your student a place to work at their own pace, tackle the things they find hardest, and celebrate the wins that come from real practice.
