§ 01

Getting started.

What happens at a trial class?
You and your child join a real class alongside current students — no separate "sample" session. Your child jumps into the rotation and gets a feel for singing, dancing, and acting side by side with the group they'd actually be joining.
What age or grade is each program for?
  • Kazoo — ages 3–6
  • Kaleo Kids — K–5th grade
  • Songwriters — 6th–12th grade
  • Group Classes — 6th grade–Adult
  • Summer Camp — K–12th grade
If you're not sure which fits, reach out, and we'll help you figure it out.
Can my child enroll in more than one program?
Absolutely — many families mix and match, especially with Kazoo's shorter 30-minute classes.
§ 02

What to expect in class.

My child has never performed before — is that okay?
That's most kids who walk through our doors. There are no auditions to join and no experience required for any program — we meet every child right where they are.
What does my child wear or bring?
Comfortable clothes they can move in. We'll let you know if a specific class calls for anything more.
Can parents watch class?
Yes — reach out to your program contact for specifics on observation and drop-off/pick-up.
§ 03

Logistics & policies.

How many sessions are there per year?
Kaleo Kids runs five sessions a year, each ending in a live stage performance. Other programs follow their own session calendars — ask when you enroll.
What if we miss a class?
Reach out to your program contact about makeup options.
What does the end-of-session performance look like?
A real stage, real lights, microphones, and a full set — built around your child's class, not a three-hour recital where they're on stage for five minutes.
§ 04

Cost & access.

For returning Kaleo Families: Why does the price look higher this session?
We added more classes to this session, so the total is higher, but the price per class is about the same as always. We also don't charge costume fees, class attire requirements (ballet shoes, leotards, dance shoes, etc.), or competition fees like many studios do, so there are no surprise add-ons later. Questions about your family's pricing? Just reach out.
What's included in the monthly rate?
Weekly class time and the end-of-session performance. We don't charge costume fees or competition entry fees — the price you see is the price you pay.
Do you offer financial assistance or scholarships?
Yes. Through our nonprofit partnership with Abide Ministries, we're committed to making sure the invitation stays open to every family regardless of financial situation. Visit our About page or reach out directly to ask about scholarship options.

Still have questions?

We're happy to talk it through. No question is too small. Reach out and we'll help you find the right fit.
